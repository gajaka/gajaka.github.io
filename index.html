Formal certification, refinement and repair 2024–2026

Lean 4 and PVS developments on certificate-preserving transitions, parametric certification, repair and progress obligations. Includes the General Lovász Local Lemma and Moser–Tardos constructive repair.

The Lean 4.35.0 library contains 1,824 explicit theorems and lemmas across 191 source modules, with no project-owned Lean axioms or sorry dependencies. PVS counterparts have been independently replayed. Builds and axiom-dependency audits are documented in the repositories.

Results apply to the formal models under their stated assumptions; preservation and repair obligations are explicit.

C buffer publication: Lean and CBMC 2026

A verification case study of a sequential, 64-byte buffer API. Eight contracts are checked with CBMC, supported by seven negative controls. Lean proofs cover its functional model and certificate-preserving controller refinement.

C verification is scoped to the declared C/library model. Source and reproducible reports are available on request.
